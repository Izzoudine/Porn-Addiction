The goal of the app is to help users gradually overcome porn addiction by reducing their viewing time and frequency step by step.

🧩 Step 1: Account Setup

When creating an account, the user is asked two questions:

Frequency – How often they watch porn per week (1–7+ times).

Duration – How long each session usually lasts (10–60 minutes).

⚙️ Phase 1: Reduce Duration per Session

The app first focuses on reducing how long the user watches.

Each week, the allowed duration per session is reduced by about one-third.

Example:

If the user starts with 60 minutes, the plan goes:

Week 1: 40 minutes

Week 2: 20 minutes

Week 3: 10 minutes

If the user starts with 50 minutes:

Week 1: 40 minutes

Week 2: 20 minutes

Week 3: 10 minutes

If the user starts with 30 minutes:

Week 1: 20 minutes

Week 2: 10 minutes

If the user starts with 20 minutes:

Week 1: 10 minutes

📱 How it works technically:

The app uses accessibility features to monitor the user’s screen.

When it detects adult content for more than 1 minute, it considers that a “session” has started.

A timer begins counting.

Once the user reaches the current week’s allowed duration, the app blocks access to adult content for the rest of that session.

🗓️ Phase 2: Reduce Weekly Frequency

Once the session duration has been reduced to 10 minutes, the next goal is to reduce how often the user watches.

If the user watches 4–7 times a week, reduce by 2 times each week until they reach 1 time per week.

If the user watches 2–3 times a week, reduce by 1 time each week until they reach 1 time per week.

📆 Phase 3: Reduce to Every 2 Weeks

When the user is down to once a week:

They move to watching once every 2 weeks.

🕊️ Phase 4: Final Reduction

Finally, the goal is to:

Move from 2 times a month → once a month → complete control (no compulsion).
